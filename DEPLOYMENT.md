Prepared by IDU House, 2026-10-07. Weeks are counted from the day Octavia says yes.

Ownership, revenue, and who pays for what are not part of this plan. Those belong in a written agreement between the two of you.

## The steps

| Phase | When | What happens | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Lock it** | Week 1 | Octavia signs off on the name, the six products, and the hero colorway. A native Japanese reader checks 大 and 大丈夫. A quick trademark search is run on THE GREAT for clothing. | Written yes from Octavia, reader confirms the Japanese, search shows no clear conflict. |
| **1. Print files** | Week 1 | Final files sized to the print area for each product in Printify. Fonts confirmed free for commercial use. Vector versions made for the lockup and lettering so embroidery and vinyl stay crisp. | Every product has a print ready file that passes the Printify preview. |
| **2. Samples** | Weeks 1 to 3 | Order one sample each of the hoodie (black), tee (cream), mug, and decal, then the pink hoodie. Judge the blank: heavy enough, boxy enough, soft enough. Check how gold and silver gradients print. Dark garments need a white base layer under the art, so we look at the real result before trusting the mockup. | Samples in hand. If a blank feels thin, we choose a heavier one and order again before selling anything. |
| **3. Store** | Weeks 2 to 3 | Build a collection called THE GREAT in the store she already runs with Printify. Replace mockups with photos of the real samples. Write the product pages in her voice. Shopify trial runs alongside as a test only, so we can compare, not as a move. | Collection live in a hidden state, test order placed and received. |
| **4. Soft launch** | Week 4 | Open to her email list and her existing audience first. One hero hits first: the Back Message Hoodie. Post a short story about who the message is for, the person behind you. | First orders shipped, and the post purchase question is live. |
| **5. Drop rhythm** | Weeks 4 to 6 | Week 4 hoodie. Week 5 tees. Week 6 mug and decal. One piece per week keeps attention on one thing at a time. | All six products live. |
| **6. Ads** | Week 5 onward | IDU House tests a small ad budget on the social media platforms that are giving natural engagement, using photos of real people wearing the back print. Budget to be agreed. | We know which message, which product, and which photo sells to strangers. |
| **7. Learn and expand** | Week 8 onward | Reorder what sells. Add a cap or tote with the crown. Open the summer drop (Pride pieces) and the fan edition mug as their own moments. Explore a personalized "[Name] the Great" tee if the store supports custom text. Explore selling inside a social shop. | A second drop on the calendar. |

## The content engine

Every piece of content comes back to one idea: the person behind you.

- **Win Wall:** buyers send a photo of the back print out in the world. Best ones go on the store and the social pages.
- **Back print reveals:** a short video of Octavia turning around to show the message.
- **Say it to a stranger:** people record themselves reading "I really want you to win" to someone. Octavia chooses the best.
- **Decals in the wild:** cars with the decal on the back window.

## How we know it is working

The goal is people who do not know her music. So the one number that matters most is how many buyers found the line without knowing her.

- A one question survey after purchase: "How did you find us?" with an option for "I saw it on someone and wanted it."
- Email sign ups from buyers, to see how many join her list through merch.
- Sell through of the hero versus the rest.
- Which photo or line gets the most strangers to click.

## Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | What we do |
|---|---|
| The sample does not feel premium | Order samples before selling. Change the blank if needed. |
| Gold and silver gradients print flat | Check the samples. Offer a solid color version if the gradient loses its shine. |
| Japanese is slightly off | Native reader check before anything is printed. Only two words are used, so the check is quick. |
| THE GREAT already belongs to someone in clothing | Trademark search in Phase 0. Backup name ready: OTG, or Octavia the Great. |
| A font license turns out to be limited | Confirm in Phase 1. Swap the font before print if needed. |
| The line turns into too many things | Six products. New ideas wait for a later drop. |
